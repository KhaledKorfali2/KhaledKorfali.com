# The doer and the done-to

Back in lesson 2 you saw that a pattern can be applied to any root. Two patterns are so regular and so common that learning them multiplies your vocabulary straight away.

## The doer: فَاعِلٌ

A long ā after the first root letter and an i after the second. It means the one who does the action.

| Root idea | Word | Meaning |
|-----------|------|---------|
| creating | خَالِقٌ | a creator |
| knowing | عَالِمٌ | one who knows |
| disbelieving | كَافِرٌ | a disbeliever |
| being truthful | صَادِقٌ | one who is truthful |
| wronging | ظَالِمٌ | a wrongdoer |

You have been using this pattern since lesson 2. مَالِكٌ in مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ is another: the one who owns.

## The done-to: مَفْعُولٌ

A مَـ at the front and a long ū after the second root letter. It means the one the action is done to.

| Root idea | Word | Meaning |
|-----------|------|---------|
| writing | مَكْتُوبٌ | written |
| knowing | مَعْلُومٌ | known |
| creating | مَخْلُوقٌ | created, a created thing |

> غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ
> not of those who have earned anger (1:7)

الْمَغْضُوب is this pattern on the root غ ض ب, anger: those upon whom there is anger.

## A pair from every verb

Any simple verb gives you both: خَلَقَ gives خَالِقٌ, the creator, and مَخْلُوقٌ, the created. Grammar itself is named this way. The doer of a verb is the فَاعِلٌ and the object is the مَفْعُولٌ, both from the root ف ع ل, "doing".

## Practice

? What does the pattern فَاعِلٌ mean?
+ The one who does the action
- The one the action is done to
- The place where the action happens
! A long ā after the first root letter: the doer.

? What does the pattern مَفْعُولٌ mean?
- The one who does the action
+ The one the action is done to
- The action itself
! مَـ at the front and a long ū: the done-to.

?sort Doer pattern or done-to pattern?
Doer: خَالِقٌ, عَالِمٌ, كَاتِبٌ, شَاهِدٌ
Done-to: مَخْلُوقٌ, مَعْلُومٌ, مَكْتُوبٌ, مَغْضُوبٌ

+ a helper

- help

? نَصَرَ means "he helped". What does مَنْصُورٌ mean?
- a helper
+ one who is helped
- he will help
! The done-to pattern on the same root.

? ذَكَرَ means "he mentioned". Which word means "mentioned"?
- ذَاكِرٌ

! مَـ and a long ū make the done-to form. ذَاكِرٌ would be "one who mentions".

## Cards

Q: What is the doer pattern, and what does it mean?
A: فَاعِلٌ: a long ā after the first root letter and an i after the second. It means the one who does the action, as in خَالِقٌ, a creator.

Q: What is the done-to pattern, and what does it mean?
A: مَفْعُولٌ: مَـ at the front and a long ū after the second root letter. It means the one the action is done to, as in مَخْلُوقٌ, created.
