# Patterns of intensity

Many of the names and descriptions of Allah in the Qur'an are built on patterns that mean more than simply "the one who does". They describe a quality that is constant, complete or often repeated. Recognising these patterns tells you something that a one-word English translation can't.

## فَعِيلٌ: a lasting quality

An a after the first root letter and a long ī after the second. It describes a quality that belongs to someone permanently.

> رَحِيمٌ    عَلِيمٌ    سَمِيعٌ    بَصِيرٌ    حَكِيمٌ    قَدِيرٌ
> merciful, all-knowing, all-hearing, all-seeing, wise, able

Compare عَالِمٌ, "one who knows", with عَلِيمٌ, "all-knowing". They share a root. The pattern makes the difference.

## فَعَّالٌ: again and again

The middle root letter is doubled and followed by a long ā. It describes doing something constantly or abundantly.

> غَفَّارٌ    تَوَّابٌ    وَهَّابٌ    رَزَّاقٌ
> ever-forgiving, ever-accepting of repentance, ever-giving, the constant provider

## فَعُولٌ: in great measure

An a after the first root letter and a long ū after the second.

> غَفُورٌ    شَكُورٌ    وَدُودٌ
> most forgiving, most appreciative, most loving

Both غَفُورٌ and غَفَّارٌ come from the root غ ف ر, forgiving. One stresses how great the forgiveness is, the other how often it is given.

## الرَّحْمَٰنُ

رَحْمَٰن is on a rarer pattern, ending in ـَان, which describes being filled to overflowing with a quality. It is used only of Allah. Together, الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ takes one root, mercy, through two patterns: mercy that is overflowing, and mercy that is lasting.

## Cards

Q: What do the patterns فَعِيلٌ, فَعَّالٌ and فَعُولٌ convey?
A: فَعِيلٌ a lasting quality (رَحِيمٌ). فَعَّالٌ doing something constantly (غَفَّارٌ). فَعُولٌ a quality in great measure (غَفُورٌ).

Q: How do الرَّحْمَٰن and الرَّحِيم differ?
A: Both are from the root ر ح م, mercy. الرَّحْمَٰن is on a pattern for being filled to overflowing. الرَّحِيم is on فَعِيلٌ, a lasting quality.
