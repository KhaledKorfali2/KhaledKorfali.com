# Verb families

A root does not make only one verb. By adding letters in fixed ways, Arabic makes a family of related verbs from the same root, each with a predictable shift in meaning. Western grammars number these forms with Roman numerals. This lesson introduces the three you will meet most.

## Form I: the simple verb

The bare root with short vowels. This is everything you learned in lessons 17 and 18.

> عَلِمَ    نَزَلَ
> he knew, he came down

## Form II: double the middle letter

Doubling the middle root letter often means **making someone else do it**, or doing it thoroughly.

> عَلِمَ    عَلَّمَ
> he knew, he taught (he made someone know)

> نَزَلَ    نَزَّلَ
> he came down, he sent down, part by part

## Form IV: add أَ at the front

An أَ before the root also means causing the action.

> نَزَلَ    أَنْزَلَ
> he came down, he sent down

> إِنَّا أَنْزَلْنَاهُ فِي لَيْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ
> Indeed, We sent it down on the Night of Decree. (97:1)

Read that verb piece by piece: أَنْزَلَ is Form IV, ـْنَا is "we", ـهُ is "it". آمَنَ, "he believed", and أَسْلَمَ, "he submitted", are Form IV as well. In the present tense, Forms II and IV begin with a u sound, which is why it is يُؤْمِنُ and not يَأْمَنُ.

## Form X: add اِسْتَـ at the front

This usually means **seeking** or asking for the thing.

> غَفَرَ    اِسْتَغْفَرَ
> he forgave, he sought forgiveness

> وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
> and You alone we ask for help (1:5)

نَسْتَعِينُ is Form X on a root meaning "help": we seek help.

## The name of the action

Each form has a matching noun that names the action. Form IV gives nouns shaped like إِيمَانٌ, "faith", and إِسْلَامٌ, "submission". Form II gives nouns shaped like تَنْزِيلٌ, "a sending down".

## Cards

Q: What do Forms II, IV and X usually add to a root's meaning?
A: Form II (doubled middle letter) and Form IV (أَ at the front) usually mean causing the action. Form X (اِسْتَـ at the front) means seeking it.

Q: Break أَنْزَلْنَاهُ into its parts.
A: أَنْزَلَ (he sent down, Form IV) + ـْنَا (we) + ـهُ (it): "We sent it down."
