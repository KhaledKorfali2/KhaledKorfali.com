# The word for "the"

Arabic has one word for "the": الـ, written joined to the front of a noun. بَيْتٌ is "a house" and الْبَيْتُ is "the house".

How you pronounce it depends on the letter that follows.

## Moon letters: the L is heard

Before about half the letters you pronounce the ل normally. These are called moon letters, after the word الْقَمَرُ (al-qamar), "the moon", where the L is clearly heard. The ل carries a small circle called a sukūn, which means "no vowel here".

> الْقَمَرُ    الْكِتَابُ    الْبَيْتُ
> al-qamar, al-kitāb, al-bayt

## Sun letters: the L disappears

Before the other letters the ل is written but not pronounced. The next letter is doubled instead, and it carries the doubling mark called a shaddah. These are called sun letters, after الشَّمْسُ (ash-shams), "the sun".

> الشَّمْسُ    النُّورُ    الرَّحِيمُ
> ash-shams, an-nūr, ar-raḥīm

The fourteen sun letters are:

> ت ث د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ل ن

You don't need to memorise the list to read. The writing tells you: a shaddah on the letter after الـ means a sun letter, and a sukūn on the ل means a moon letter.

## Practice

? How is الشَّمْسُ pronounced?
- al-shams
+ ash-shams
- a-shams
! ش is a sun letter. The ل is silent and the ش is doubled.

? How is الْقَمَرُ pronounced?
+ al-qamar
- aq-qamar
- a-qamar
! ق is a moon letter, so the ل is heard.

?sort Sun letter or moon letter? Look at the letter right after الـ.
Sun: الشَّمْسُ, النُّورُ, السَّمَاءُ, الرَّحِيمُ
Moon: الْقَمَرُ, الْبَيْتُ, الْأَرْضُ, الْيَوْمُ
! A shaddah after الـ means the ل is silent. A sukūn on the ل means it is heard.

? When you are reading, what tells you that a word begins with a sun letter?
+ A shaddah on the letter after الـ
- A sukūn on the ل
- A long vowel at the end
! The doubling mark shows that the ل has merged into the next letter.

? What does النُّورُ mean?
- a light
+ the light
- lights
! نُورٌ is "light" and الـ makes it definite: an-nūr.
