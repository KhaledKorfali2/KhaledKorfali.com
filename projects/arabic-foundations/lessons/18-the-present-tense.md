# The present tense

The present tense covers "he does", "he is doing" and often "he will do". Where the past tense marks who did it with an ending, the present marks it mainly with a **letter at the start**.

## Four starting letters

| Starts with | Who | Example | Meaning |
|-------------|-----|---------|---------|
| يَـ | he, they | يَعْلَمُ | he knows |
| تَـ | you, she | تَعْلَمُ | you know, she knows |
| أَ | I | أَعْلَمُ | I know |
| نَـ | we | نَعْلَمُ | we know |

For a group, the ending ـُونَ is added as well:

| Arabic | Say | Meaning |
|--------|-----|---------|
| يَعْلَمُونَ | yaʿlamūna | they know |
| تَعْلَمُونَ | taʿlamūna | you (a group) know |

So when you meet a present-tense verb, read the first letter for who, and check the end for whether it is a group.

## In the Qur'an

> إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
> You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help. (1:5)

Both verbs start with نَـ: "we".

> الَّذِينَ يُؤْمِنُونَ بِالْغَيْبِ
> those who believe in the unseen (2:3)

> لَا أَعْبُدُ مَا تَعْبُدُونَ
> I do not worship what you worship. (109:2)

One root, ع ب د, appears twice in that last verse: with أَ for "I", and with تَـ and ـُونَ for "you all".

## The middle vowel

The vowel on the middle root letter varies from verb to verb: يَعْلَمُ, يَعْبُدُ, يَعْمَلُ. It has to be learned with each verb, and a dictionary gives it. Some verbs also have u on the first letter instead of a, as in يُؤْمِنُ. Lesson 23 explains why.

## Cards

Q: What are the four letters that start a present-tense verb, and who does each mark?
A: يَـ (he, they), تَـ (you, she), أَ (I), نَـ (we).

Q: How is a group shown in the present tense?
A: With the ending ـُونَ, as in يَعْلَمُونَ (they know) and تَعْلَمُونَ (you all know).
