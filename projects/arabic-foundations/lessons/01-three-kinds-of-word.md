# Three kinds of word

Every word in Arabic belongs to one of three groups. Knowing which group a word is in tells you what it can do in a sentence, so this is the first thing Arabic grammar asks about any word.

## The three groups

| Group | Arabic name | What it is |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| Noun | اِسْمٌ | A person, place, thing, idea or quality. English nouns, adjectives and pronouns all count as this. |
| Verb | فِعْلٌ | An action tied to a time: past, present or a command. |
| Particle | حَرْفٌ | A small word that only has meaning next to other words, like "in", "and" or "not". |

Notice that the first group is wider than the English word "noun". In Arabic grammar *big*, *he* and *this* are all kinds of اِسْمٌ.

## How to tell them apart

- If it can take "the" in front, it is a noun. الْكِتَابُ means "the book".
- If it tells you something happened and when, it is a verb. كَتَبَ means "he wrote".
- If it is a short connecting word that can't stand alone, it is a particle. وَ means "and".

> كَتَبَ الرَّجُلُ الْكِتَابَ
> The man wrote the book. A verb, then two nouns.

Don't worry yet about the small marks at the ends of words, such as the doubled mark on كِتَابٌ. Lesson 4 explains them.

## Words

| حَرْفٌ | ḥarf | particle | Also means "a letter" of the alphabet |

## Practice

? Which group does كَتَبَ ("he wrote") belong to?
- اِسْمٌ
+ فِعْلٌ
- حَرْفٌ
! It names an action and places it in the past, so it is a verb.

? In Arabic grammar, which group would the word "big" belong to?
+ اِسْمٌ
- فِعْلٌ
- حَرْفٌ
! Adjectives are a kind of اِسْمٌ. The group covers nouns, adjectives and pronouns.

? What is the quickest test for whether a word is a noun?
+ It can take "the" in front of it

- It comes first in the sentence

## Cards

Q: What are the three kinds of word in Arabic?
