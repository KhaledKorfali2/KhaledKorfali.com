# Commands, and saying no

Two things remain before you can read most verbs you meet: how to tell someone to do something, and how to say that something does not happen.

## Commands

The command is made from the present tense by removing the letter at the start. The final vowel is dropped too.

> قُلْ
> Say! From يَقُولُ, "he says".

> اِقْرَأْ
> Read! From يَقْرَأُ, "he reads". (96:1)

If removing the first letter leaves a word that starts with two consonants, a helping alif is put in front so that it can be said, as in اِقْرَأْ.

A command to a group ends in ـُوا: اُعْبُدُوا, "worship!"

> اِهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ
> Guide us to the straight path. (1:6)

اِهْدِ is the command "guide", and ـنَا is the attached pronoun "us".

## Saying no

Arabic uses a different word for "not" depending on the time it refers to.

| Word | Used with | Meaning | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|---------|
| لَا | the present | does not | لَا يَعْلَمُونَ, they do not know |
| لَمْ | the present | did not | لَمْ يَلِدْ, He did not beget |
| لَنْ | the present | will never | لَنْ يَخْلُقُوا, they will never create |
| مَا | the past | did not | مَا كَفَرَ, he did not disbelieve |

The surprising one is لَمْ. It is followed by a present-tense verb, but the meaning is past.

> لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
> He neither begets nor is born. (112:3)

لَا before a "you" verb is also how you tell someone not to do something:

> لَا تَحْزَنْ
> Do not grieve. (9:40)

## Cards

Q: What are the four words for "not", and when is each used?
A: لَا with the present (does not), لَمْ with the present but meaning the past (did not), لَنْ with the present for the future (will never), and مَا with the past (did not).

Q: How do you say "do not ..." to someone?
A: لَا followed by a "you" verb, as in لَا تَحْزَنْ, "do not grieve".
