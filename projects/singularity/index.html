Singularity

Everything pulls on everything

Every body here attracts every other one with Newton's law of gravity, worked out for each pair many times a second:

F = G · m1 · m2 / r²

Tap to drop something into a circular orbit around whatever is pulling hardest there. Drag to throw it, and the dotted line shows where it is headed. Pinch or scroll to zoom.

Bodies that touch merge. Enough planets clumped together ignite into a star. Anything crossing a black hole's edge is gone, and the hole gets heavier.

Want a goal? Slingshot is a game built on the same gravity: one launch, twelve levels.

The warping is real physics too. A black hole bends light passing near it, so the stars and planets behind it are smeared into arcs: gravitational lensing. Two black holes that get close lose energy, spiral together and merge, sending a ripple through space.
