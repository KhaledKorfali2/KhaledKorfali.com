Slingshot

One push, then gravity does the rest

Drag anywhere to move the arrow: its direction is where the probe will go and its length is how fast. Then press Launch. After that you can't steer: every planet and black hole pulls on it, and its path is whatever falls out of that.

The arrow stays where you left it, so after a miss you can change it a little and try again. On a keyboard, the arrow keys nudge it and Space launches; hold Shift for finer steps.

Fly it into the ring to finish the level. The ring has a weak pull of its own inside the faint dotted circle, so a near miss is drawn in. Pick up the three stars on the way for a full score. Hitting a planet or a red barrier, crossing a black hole's edge or leaving the dotted boundary sends the probe back to the start.

The dotted line shows only the first second of the flight. The faint trails are your last three attempts.

This is how real spacecraft travel. A probe that passes close behind a moving planet is flung onward faster, a gravity assist, which is how Voyager reached the outer planets.
