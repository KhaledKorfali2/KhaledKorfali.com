Epicycles

1 circle

Any shape is a sum of circles

Your drawing is treated as one closed loop. A Fourier transform breaks that loop into a set of circles, each with its own size, speed and starting angle.

z(t) = Σ ck · e2πi·k·t

Chain the circles tip to tail, spin each at a whole-number speed, and the last tip retraces what you drew. The big slow circles carry the overall shape; the tiny fast ones add corners and detail.

Drag the Circles slider down to see how few it takes before your drawing is still recognisable. The trefoil preset needs only two.

Draw any shape. The circles will learn to redraw it.
